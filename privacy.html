Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 August 2026. Placeholder wording for this demo site.

1. Who we are

Murdock’s Butchery & Goods is a butchery in Kaitaia, Northland. This policy explains what we do with personal information collected through this website and through orders and enquiries, and follows the Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect

This site has no contact form, so we only hold what you choose to give us.

  • When you call or email: your name, phone number or email address, and whatever you tell us about your order.
  • Order details: what you asked for, quantities, and pickup arrangements, so we can get it right.
  • Basic website data: standard technical information such as browser type and pages visited, collected by our hosting provider for security and performance.

3. How we use it

We use your information to answer your enquiry, prepare and hand over your order, sort out any problem with it, and keep our own records. We do not sell it, rent it, or trade it to anyone.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it is needed to run the business, for example with our website host or payment provider, or where the law requires it. Those providers may store data outside New Zealand under their own privacy obligations.

5. Third party services on this site

This site embeds a Google Map and links out to Google Maps, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. When you use those, the relevant provider’s own privacy policy applies to what they collect. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and order information for as long as it is needed to serve you and to meet business and tax record keeping requirements, then dispose of it securely.

7. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No system is completely secure, but access to customer details is limited to people who need it to do their job.

8. Your rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you and ask for it to be corrected. Contact the shop and we will deal with your request as quickly as we reasonably can.

9. Cookies

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies of its own. Embedded Google content may set cookies in your browser, and you can block or clear these in your browser settings.

10. Contact

Privacy questions can go to the shop on 09 408 1252 or by email.
