About the shop

A butcher’s counter, not a supermarket chiller.

Murdock’s Butchery & Goods is built around the counter. You point at what you want, say how you want it cut, and it gets done in front of you. Nothing sits pre packed and forgotten under a light for three days.

Same thinking goes for the goods on the shelf. They are there because they go with what is in the cabinet, so you can get the whole feed sorted in one stop instead of driving to the other end of town for a bottle of sauce.

  • Cut to order while you wait, not pre packed
  • Locally owned and run here in Kaitaia
  • Ring ahead and we will put it aside for you
